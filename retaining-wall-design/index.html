A basement excavation in Edinburgh’s New Town district recently hit a buried glacial till layer that shifted the entire retaining wall design. We had to switch from a simple cantilever to a tied-back system. That is the reality of working in this city. Every project brings its own ground conditions. We provide retaining wall design in Edinburgh that accounts for these surprises. Before we finalise any scheme, we always run a placa de carga to verify bearing capacity at formation level. It saves costly redesigns later.

In Edinburgh, the water table often controls the wall height. One metre of extra head can double the required base width.

Scope of work in Edinburgh

The geology under Edinburgh is a mix of Carboniferous sedimentary rocks overlain by glacial deposits. The water table sits high in many areas, especially near the Water of Leith. For retaining wall design in Edinburgh, we must handle both drained and undrained conditions.
  • We assess soil shear strength using corte-directo to define c' and phi' for long-term stability.
  • We check groundwater pressures with standpipes before setting wall dimensions.
Our team uses limit-state design per Eurocode 7. We size stems, bases, and reinforcement to resist overturning and sliding. Every design includes a full bearing capacity check against the founding stratum.

Typical technical challenges in Edinburgh

Compare a wall in Morningside versus one in Leith. Morningside has dense glacial till with low permeability. Leith has soft alluvial clays and a high water table. The same wall section fails in one but works in the other. That is why generic designs do not hold in Edinburgh. We tailor every retaining wall design in Edinburgh to the specific ground profile. We also check for long-term creep in soft clays and potential for basal heave in deep excavations.

Our services

We offer two main services for retaining wall design in Edinburgh.

Cantilever and Gravity Wall Design

Reinforced concrete cantilever or mass gravity walls for cuts up to 4 m. We size the base, check sliding, overturning, and bearing. Suitable for glacial till and rock sites.

Anchored and Soil-Nailed Wall Design

Tied-back walls or soil-nailed slopes for deeper excavations or restricted sites. We design anchor lengths, bond zones, and nail patterns. Common in Leith and around the city centre.

FAQ

What soil parameters do you need for a retaining wall design in Edinburgh?

We need the effective cohesion (c') and friction angle (phi') from site-specific direct shear or triaxial tests. We also require the unit weight, water table level, and undrained shear strength if clays are present. These come from our site investigation.

How much does retaining wall design cost in Edinburgh?

Typical design fees range from £730 to £2.900 depending on wall height, soil complexity, and number of sections. The exact cost depends on the site investigation scope and the wall type selected.

Do you handle basement walls in Edinburgh’s conservation areas?

Yes. We design walls that meet both structural requirements and planning constraints. We work with temporary works to minimise excavation width and use anchored systems where space is tight.

What is the biggest risk for retaining walls in Edinburgh?

High groundwater pressure, especially after heavy rain. A wall designed for drained conditions can fail if the drains block. We always include a drainage layer and weep holes, and we check for hydrostatic uplift on the base slab.
